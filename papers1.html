About this Edition

ALORE: ILORIN JOURNAL OF THE HUMANITIES is a publication of the Faculty of Arts, University of Ilorin. It is a peer reviewed Journal established to provide academics and researchers an outlet to publish their latest research findings in their chosen fields of studies.

ALORE: ILORIN JOURNAL OF THE HUMANITIES reviewers are scholars selected by the Editorial Board to review manuscripts on the basis of their expertise in particular content areas of their fields of specialisation. Decision regarding the publication of manuscripts is based on their recommendations.

ALORE: ILORIN JOURNAL OF THE HUMANITIES accepts and publishes solicited and unsolicited articles annually. It, therefore, invites high quality researched papers/articles, book reviews and creative literary works in Arts, Humanities and other academic fields which research findings are original contributions to knowledge and are meant to impact the society positively.

ALORE: ILORIN JOURNAL OF THE HUMANITIES is self-sponsored; contributing authors will be expected to pay a token as may be decided by the Editorial Board.
