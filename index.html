About the Journal

Alore: Ilorin Journal of the Humanities is a scholarly, peer-reviewed academic journal dedicated to the advancement of research, critical inquiry, and intellectual discourse in the humanities. The journal provides a platform for scholars, researchers, academics, and emerging researchers to publish original and significant contributions that engage with contemporary and enduring issues across the broad field of the humanities.

The journal welcomes well-researched articles, critical essays, theoretical studies, reviews, and other scholarly contributions that demonstrate academic rigor, originality, and relevance. Its scope encompasses diverse areas of the humanities, including but not limited to Languages and Linguistics, Literature, History, Philosophy, Religious and Cultural Studies, Theatre and Performing Arts, Communication, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Alore: Ilorin Journal of the Humanities is committed to maintaining high standards of academic integrity and scholarly excellence. All submissions are subjected to a rigorous peer-review process designed to ensure the quality, originality, and scholarly relevance of published works. The journal upholds the principles of responsible research and publication, including originality, proper attribution, transparency, and respect for intellectual property.

Through the dissemination of quality research, the journal seeks to contribute to the growth of knowledge, encourage meaningful scholarly dialogue, and promote a deeper understanding of human society, culture, language, history, thought, and creative expression.

The journal welcomes contributions from scholars and researchers within Nigeria and across the international academic community. By fostering interdisciplinary engagement and providing a credible avenue for the dissemination of humanities research, Alore: Ilorin Journal of the Humanities aims to contribute meaningfully to academic development and the advancement of knowledge.

Manuscript Preparation Guidelines

- Manuscripts, prepared in in Microsoft Word format should be original, unpublished, and not under consideration by any other journal.

- Manuscripts should be double-spaced, with Times New Roman font, 12 points size, and margins of 1 inch on all sides.

- The title page should contain the full names of the author(s), institutional affiliation and a very short biodata. Note that the first page of the main text should begin with the title of the article (repeated) just before the abstract. However, the name and address of the author(s) should not reflect on this page for the purpose of blind review...

Editorial Board

The Editorial Board of the Alóre: Ilorin Journal of the Humanities, published by the Faculty of Arts, University of Ilorin in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria, is composed of experienced scholars and researchers committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic excellence, integrity, and ethical publishing. The Board oversees the scholarly direction and quality of the journal by ensuring that all submitted manuscripts undergo a rigorous peer-review process that promotes originality, critical inquiry, and intellectual contribution to the arts and humanities. Through this process, the Editorial Board strives to provide a credible platform for scholars, researchers, and practitioners from Nigeria and the international academic community to disseminate innovative research, encourage interdisciplinary dialogue, and contribute meaningfully to the advancement of knowledge within the humanities.

Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to make a submission to this journal. All submissions will be assessed by an editor to determine whether they meet the aims and scope of this journal. Those considered to be a good fit will be sent for peer review before determining whether they will be accepted or rejected.
