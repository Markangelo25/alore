CONTEXTUAL INTERPRETATION OF 1 PETER 3:3-4 AND ITS IMPACT ON THE SEVENTH-DAY ADVENTIST CHURCH IN OKE-ODE, KWARA CONFERENCE

Author: John Yahaya Adewuyi

Date: 2026/01/10

Abstract

This study examines the contextual interpretation of 1Peter 3:3-4 and its specific impact on the Seventh-day Adventist (SDA) Church in Oke-Ode, Kwara Conference, Nigeria. The passage, which contrasts outward adornment (elaborate hair, gold, fine apparel) with the “incorruptible beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit,” forms the foundation of the SDA Church's traditional stance on dress and personal appearance. While the denomination advocates for simplicity globally, the application of this text in the unique Nigerian socio-cultural context often leads to tension. Employing a mixed-methods approach combining historical-grammatical exegesis with a localized descriptive survey of the OkeOde congregation, this research first establishes the probable first-century meaning of Peter's counsel (stressing priority of character over prohibition of all adornment). The study then analyzes how local church leaders and members in Oke-Ode interpret and enforce this teaching, navigating the conflict between SDA theological heritage and local cultural aesthetics, where dress and certain non-prohibited jewelry may hold significant social value. The findings reveal that a highly literal and legalistic interpretation of the text creates notable challenges, including internal dissension between generations, the perception of cultural irrelevance among non-members, and potential barriers to evangelism. The paper concludes that a principle-driven, contextually informed interpretation, focusing on the development of the “hidden person of the heart” as the primary form of Christian witness, is essential for promoting church unity and maximizing missioners effectiveness within the Oke-Ode community. Recommendations are provided for leadership to harmonize biblical authority, denominational standards, and cultural sensitivity.

Keywords:1Peter 3:3-4, Seventh-day Adventist Church, Adornment, Contextual Interpretation, Kwara Conference, Oke-Ode, Mission, Dress, Legalism, Cultural Relevance.
