Call for Papers & Notes for Contributors

Scholars and researchers are cordially invited to submit well-researched manuscripts for publication in Alore: Ilorin Journal of the Humanities. The journal published annually by Faculty of Arts, University of Ilorin, Ilorin, Nigeria. The journal is dedicated to publishing high-quality research articles, reviews, and essays in the field of humanities.

Alore is an interdisciplinary journal that welcomes submissions in, but not restricted to, the following areas:

- Literature and Language Studies

- History and Cultural Studies

- Ethics, Peace and Security Studies

- Art, Music, and Performance Studies

- Religion and Anthropology

- Communication and Translation
